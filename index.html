FAQ

Before you order.

The useful answers, without sixteen paragraphs of corporate fog.

How fast is delivery?

Small packs target a 24–48 hour turnaround after the brief is confirmed. More complex custom requests may take longer, and timing is confirmed before payment.

What counts as one revision?

One revision round means one collected set of reasonable changes to the agreed concept, such as text, spacing, color balance or small visual adjustments. A completely new direction is a new concept.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled separately after the project details are confirmed, using Revolut / Revolut Pro. The order form itself does not charge you.

What file formats do I get?

Final assets are supplied in platform-ready image formats such as PNG or JPG, depending on the asset and platform requirements.

Can I send references or screenshots?

Yes. The order form accepts links, notes and optional file uploads so you can show exactly what you like, dislike or want to match stylistically.

Do I need to know exactly what style I want?

No. Pick the closest direction, choose “custom,” or describe the mood, game, colors and creator identity. We can shape the visual direction from there.
