FAQ

Before you send the brief.

The useful answers, minus the agency fog machine.

Why is the first thumbnail $19?

It is a paid trial designed to test fit on a real video without forcing a larger commitment. If the working relationship fits, packs reduce the per-thumbnail cost.

Do you guarantee CTR?

No. Thumbnail design can improve clarity and packaging, but CTR also depends on topic, title, audience, impressions and platform distribution. We do not fabricate performance guarantees.

How fast is delivery?

The target is 24–48 hours after the brief is confirmed. More complex work or larger batches can take longer and timing is agreed before payment.

What counts as one revision?

One collected set of reasonable changes to the agreed direction, such as text, crop, color balance or composition adjustments. A completely new concept is a new direction.

Can you also do banners and stream graphics?

Yes. Branding remains available as an add-on after the thumbnail trial or as a separate custom request.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled separately after project confirmation through Revolut / Revolut Pro. The brief form itself does not charge you.
